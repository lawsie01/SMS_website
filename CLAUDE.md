# Copy rules for this site

Audience: civil contractors, water utility asset engineers, council infrastructure
staff. They buy on spec compliance, lead time and whether the thing will pass
inspection. They do not care about "innovation" or "partnership".

Never use: solutions, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, elevate, unlock, empower,
leverage, tailored, bespoke, world-class, industry-leading, trusted partner,
peace of mind, commitment to excellence, journey, landscape, in today's,
that's where we come in, whether you're X or Y, we don't just X we Y, it's not
just about X it's about Y.

Never use em dashes. Never bold words for emphasis.

Banned structures:
- Rhetorical question as an opener
- Lists of three adjectives
- Paragraphs that all run the same length
- A closing sentence that restates the heading
- Headings phrased as "Something: Something Else"

Style: Australian English, plain, declarative. Vary sentence length, including
some very short ones. Say the specific thing rather than the category of
thing. If you cannot state a fact, cut the sentence rather than padding it.
